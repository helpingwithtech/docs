<Update label="2026-08-28--version-1-6-0" description="Chrome Extension: version 1.6.0 — pick exactly which bills to collect, tick them on the page itself, and narrow a run to invoices or receipts" tags={["Chrome Extension", "Help Docs"]}>

**28 August 2026** · **Chrome Extension** · **Help Docs**

**You can now take some of the bills on a page instead of all of them.** A supplier's billing portal listing fifty-four invoices used to be one button: everything, or nothing. There are now three rungs on that ladder, and you only climb as far as you need.

- **Invoices only** and **Receipts only** are back, as chips on the approval card. Tap one and the card tells you what it means for this page, as *12 of the 54 are invoices*. The choice is applied on your own computer when you approve, and the assistant is never told which one you picked.
- **Pick documents** opens a list that starts **empty**, with **Select all** at the top. Taking three of fifty-four should cost three taps, not fifty-one. The card keeps count as you go: *Nothing picked yet - Approve gets all 54*, then *3 of the 54 picked*.
- **Anything eggz already holds is greyed out and says "Already in eggz"** in that list. It cannot be ticked, and **Select all** leaves it out, because fetching it again would be free anyway.

**On a Stripe billing portal, the picker also draws checkboxes on the page itself**, against the real rows, next to the dates and amounts you are already reading. Click one row, **Shift-click** another, and everything between them is selected — which is how you take "everything since June" without anybody having to read a date. The boxes and the card's list are one selection, so ticking a row moves the count on the card. Everywhere else the card's own list is the picker and does the same job.

**The approval card no longer names a price.** It reads *54 invoices and receipts on this page*, and where it can it adds what is actually new: *3 new, 51 you already have*. Cost is stated once, after the run, where it is true.

**Smaller things worth knowing:**

- **The toolbar button is an on/off switch, not a door switcher.** Whichever door is open, the button closes it. Nothing open, and it opens whichever one **Open as** names. On a page that cannot host a card it opens the panel rather than doing nothing. A keyboard shortcut now goes to the door that is already open instead of raising a second one. See [Two ways to open it](/help/unscrambled-tabs/dashboard-and-side-panel#the-toolbar-button).
- **The bill banner stays until you close it.** It used to hide itself after a few seconds, which meant it could vanish off the screen of somebody who was reading the very page it was about. The **✕** is the only thing that closes it now.
- **A waiting approval card survives more.** Switching between Workspaces, Search and the chat no longer loses it, and a finished collect now stays in the chat it belongs to instead of turning up in a new one.
- **Enter sends the message in the side-panel composer**, and a send that is refused says why.
- **A run that is stopped, or one that runs out of credits part way, reports honestly**: what uploaded, what did not and why, and that nothing was spent on the rest.

**New help page:** [Collect every bill on a page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/collect-bills) covers the offer, the three ways to narrow it, what a run costs, and what it will not do.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-07-14--same-purchase-and-final-say" description="Web App — two documents for one purchase get combined and counted once, you can override any classification, and eggz flags weak GST paperwork before audit time" tags={["Web App"]}>

**14 July 2026** · **Web App**

- **Two documents, one purchase.** An invoice and its payment receipt are the same purchase, and eggz now treats them that way: clear matches are **combined** automatically and counted once, with both files kept and a one-click **Separate** to undo. Borderline matches become a **"Looks like the same purchase"** card showing both documents and what they share, and you answer **Yes, same purchase** or **No, keep separate**. This works across sources too, so a receipt you photograph can match an invoice that arrived by email. See [Two documents, one purchase](/help/guides/receipts-and-bills#two-documents-one-purchase).
- **Proof of payment arrived.** When a receipt matches an invoice still marked unpaid, the card says so, and combining keeps both files, marks the invoice paid, and counts the purchase once. A receipt attached to a bill now reads **Proof of payment** in its Process panel, and **Separate from bill** replaces the old Unlink wording.
- **Purchases to review, on your dashboard.** Admins see pending same-purchase suggestions as a card on the dashboard home, so a couple of taps clears them. The card only appears when there's something to review. See [Purchases to review](/help/guides/dashboard#purchases-to-review).
- **You always have the final say.** eggz's classification is an editable default, never a verdict. The receipt page adds **This is actually a bill** (with an Undo) and a one-click paid ⇄ unpaid switch. When a change has a real consequence, such as a possible double-count or a paid-status conflict, an orange note explains it first; it advises and never blocks. See [You always have the final say](/help/guides/receipts-and-bills#you-always-have-the-final-say).
- **Weak GST paperwork gets flagged early.** A purchase over A$82.50 whose document is missing an ABN or a GST breakdown may not substantiate a GST credit at audit. eggz now shows a calm standing note on such receipts so you can ask the supplier for a proper tax invoice while it's still easy. See [The GST substantiation note](/help/guides/receipts-and-bills#the-gst-substantiation-note).
- **Pick a currency when you create documents.** Invoice, bill, quote, and customer forms now have a **Currency** picker, defaulting to your reporting currency, and line items and totals follow the currency you choose.
- **Smaller fixes.** Your **Billed / Collected / Outstanding** choice now sticks for the browser session; voided documents consistently show **$0 due**; and the mobile menu's **Home** and **Approvals** items now open their pages.

</Update>

### For maintainers (entry format)

Use one `<Update>` per ship batch. **Entries describe what changed for the person reading, and nothing else.** No store submissions, no review timelines, no "on its way", no packaging or signing steps — release logistics are not a changelog entry. **`label`** — unique anchor, e.g. `YYYY-MM-DD--topic` or `ext-vMAJOR-MINOR-PATCH` when no calendar date exists. **`description`** — short headline for the timeline. **`tags`** — only **Help Docs**, **Web App**, and/or **Chrome Extension** (same order as the bold line: Help Docs → Web App → Chrome Extension). Put the **calendar date** and scopes on the first line inside the block, as in the entries above.
