Eggheadz is the team you ask. Open the panel, ask in your own words, and the answer arrives while the page you were on stays where it is.

## The panel sits on top of your page

Click **Ask eggheadz** in the top bar and the panel opens over the right-hand side of the screen. Your page does not get narrower, and nothing behind the panel is dimmed. You can still click, scroll and type on the page while an answer is on screen, so you can read a bill and ask about it at the same time.

| What you want | How |
|---------------|-----|
| **Open the panel** | Click **Ask eggheadz** in the top bar |
| **Ask from anywhere, without the mouse** | Command+J on a Mac, Ctrl+J on Windows and Linux. A box opens where you type your question, and the answer arrives in the panel |
| **Close it** | Click the **x** at the top of the panel, or press **Esc** while your cursor is inside it |

Esc only closes the panel when you are working inside it. If you are typing on the page behind, Esc belongs to the page.

**Make it wider or narrower.** Drag the left edge of the panel. Hover over it and the edge thickens and turns gold so you can see what you are about to grab.

- **Anywhere from 320 to 640 pixels wide.** Past either end it slows and springs back.
- **It settles on three widths** as you pass them: 360, 400 and 480.
- **Double-click the edge** to put it back to 400.
- **Or use the keyboard.** Tab to the edge, then press the left and right arrow keys to move it 40 pixels at a time. Home makes it as narrow as it goes, End as wide.

**It remembers your width.** The width you chose comes back next time, and so does whether you left the panel open. It is remembered for you, not for the computer, so two people signing in on the same machine each get their own panel back.

<Note>
In a private browsing window, or with site storage turned off, the panel opens at 400 pixels every time. Everything else works normally.
</Note>

**On a phone or a narrow window** there is no room to read a page and a conversation side by side. Below 1024 pixels wide the panel slides in from the right and covers the page. Close it to get back to what you were doing.

## Name a record with @

Type **@** in the ask line and a menu opens over it. Keep typing to search your suppliers, customers, bills, invoices, quotes, receipts and months.

- **Up and down** move through the matches. **Left and right** switch between the kinds, so you can jump from suppliers to months.
- **Enter** picks the highlighted one, or click it.
- The name becomes a **chip** in your question, marked with what it is, so **@Bunnings Warehouse** and **@August 2026** each say what they are.
- **Backspace** right after a chip removes the whole chip. So does the **×** on it.

The answer is about the record you named. *"How much did we spend with @Bunnings Warehouse?"* is answered for that supplier, not for one with a similar name, and *"what came in during @August 2026?"* reads that month.

@ works in the ask line of the panel and in the conversation on a record page.
