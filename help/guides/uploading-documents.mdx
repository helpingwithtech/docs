## How document upload works

eggz uses AI to automatically extract data from your receipts and bills. Upload a document, and eggz reads the merchant name, date, amounts, line items, and categories -- no manual data entry needed.

## Supported file types

| Format | Extensions | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| PDF | `.pdf` | Single and multi-page supported. A multi-page PDF is one upload and uses one document credit. |
| JPEG | `.jpg`, `.jpeg` | Photos of receipts work well |
| PNG | `.png` | Screenshots and scanned documents |
| WebP | `.webp` | Compressed web images |

<Warning>Other file types (Word documents, Excel files, HEIC photos, GIFs, TIFFs) are not supported. Convert them to PDF or JPEG before uploading.</Warning>

## Upload methods

### Drag and drop (desktop)

The fastest way to upload on desktop:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the upload area">
    The drag-and-drop zone appears on the **Dashboard** (home page) and the **Expenses** page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drag files from your computer">
    Drag one or more files from Finder or Explorer onto the upload zone. The zone highlights when files are hovering over it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drop to upload">
    Release the files. eggz immediately starts processing each document.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>You can upload multiple files at once. Each file is processed independently.</Tip>

### File picker (desktop and mobile)

Click the upload zone to open your system file picker. Select one or more files and confirm.

### Camera capture (mobile)

On mobile, use the camera button in the bottom navigation dock:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the camera button">
    The camera icon sits between Home and Expenses in the mobile bottom dock.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take a photo">
    Point your camera at a receipt or bill and capture the image. eggz converts the photo to JPEG for processing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm upload">
    The captured image uploads automatically. If you prefer, tap **Upload from Photos Instead** to pick an existing image from your gallery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### From the eggz Tab Manager extension {#from-the-unscrambled-tabs-extension}

If you use the **eggz Tab Manager** Chrome extension with a [connected eggz account](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account), you can upload without opening the web app:

- **Upload document** in the side panel — PDF, JPEG, PNG, or WebP up to 10MB.
- **Send tab screenshot** — captures the page you're viewing (for example an invoice in webmail) and uploads it as an image.

Extension uploads use the **same document credits and pipeline** as the dashboard — files appear in eggz like any other upload, and extraction starts automatically. When the extension collects a document from a website that eggz already holds from that same website, it is not uploaded or charged again — see [How document credits are counted](#how-document-credits-are-counted).

<Tip>Don't have it yet? [Add eggz Tab Manager to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/unscrambled-tabs/gfjdjhpcemmakhoaggnihfcohkmfcfcc). It's free, and it can even [capture bills from any page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/capture-bills) you're browsing.</Tip>

### Email forwarding

Forward a receipt or bill to your workspace's own eggz address and each attachment arrives as a pending receipt automatically — no app to open. See [Email-in: forward receipts to eggz](/help/guides/email-in) for how to get your address and who's allowed to send.

## What happens after upload

<Steps>
  <Step title="Document stored">
    The file is securely stored in eggz and a new receipt record is created with status **Pending**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="AI extraction">
    eggz uses AI (powered by Claude) to read the document and extract:

    - Merchant/vendor name
    - Transaction date
    - Total amount and currency
    - Individual line items with quantities and prices
    - Expense category
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review extracted data">
    Once processing completes, the receipt status changes to **Processed**. Open the receipt to review and correct any extracted data before syncing to Xero.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Publishing to Xero

After a receipt is processed, you can publish it to Xero from the **Publish to Xero** card on its detail page — you review the pre-filled details, then approve. eggz creates the appropriate record based on whether the receipt is paid or unpaid:

| Payment State | Xero Record Type | What it creates |
|--------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Unpaid** | Bill (ACCPAY) | A purchase invoice in Xero for your supplier |
| **Paid** | Spend Money (Bank Transaction) | A bank transaction in Xero. Requires selecting a bank account. |

<Note>Each receipt can only be published to Xero once. Make sure the extracted data is correct before publishing.</Note>

See [Publish a receipt to Xero](/help/guides/expenses#publish-a-receipt-to-xero) for the full walkthrough — when the card appears, choosing accounts, and what happens after publishing.

## Document limits

Your plan sets how many documents eggz processes for you. Your live usage is on **Settings → Billing** in the web app, which shows how many documents you have left and when the allowance resets.

| Plan | Document allowance | AI assistant | Billed |
|------|--------------------|--------------|--------|
| Free | 50 documents, granted once (the allowance does not refill) | Included | Free |
| Solo | 20 documents per month | Included | Monthly or yearly |
| Growth | 100 documents per month | Included | Monthly or yearly |
| Scale | Unlimited | Included | Monthly or yearly |

Plan names and prices are as shown on **Settings → Billing**. Yearly plans are billed once a year and have the same monthly document allowance as the monthly plan — there is no separate yearly cap.

### How document credits are counted {#how-document-credits-are-counted}

- **One upload, one document credit.** Every file you upload, capture or forward is one document, however many pages it has. A 3-page PDF uses one credit, not three.
- **A document eggz already holds is not charged again.** When the [eggz Tab Manager](/help/unscrambled-tabs/capture-bills) extension collects a document from a website — for example when you collect a billing portal you have collected before — and eggz already holds that document from that same website, it is not uploaded or charged again. The match is the same document from the same website; a file you upload again by hand from your computer is a new upload and counts.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My document failed to process">
    Check that the file is a supported format (PDF, JPEG, PNG, or WebP) and that the image is clear and readable. Blurry photos or very low-resolution images may fail to extract correctly. Try re-uploading a clearer version.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The extracted data is wrong">
    AI extraction is not perfect. Open the receipt detail page and edit any incorrect fields before publishing to Xero. Common issues include misread dates, incorrect totals on faded receipts, and missing line items on complex multi-page documents.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I've hit my document limit">
    Your plan has a document allowance. Upgrade your plan from **Settings > Billing** to increase it. Documents uploaded after hitting the limit will not be processed until the allowance resets or after upgrading.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a multi-page PDF use more than one credit?">
    No. One upload is one document and uses one document credit, however many pages it has. See [How document credits are counted](#how-document-credits-are-counted).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Am I charged again for a document eggz already has?">
    Not when the eggz Tab Manager extension collects it from a website eggz already holds it from — the same document from the same website is recognised and not uploaded or charged again. A file you upload again by hand from your computer is a new upload and counts. See [How document credits are counted](#how-document-credits-are-counted).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't publish to Xero">
    Ensure your Xero connection is active in **Settings > Integrations**. The receipt must be in **Processed** status before it can be published. If publishing a paid receipt as Spend Money, you must select a bank account first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
