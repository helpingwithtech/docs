## The toolbar button opens a side panel, not the card

That's expected on pages Chrome won't let extensions draw on: its own `chrome://` settings pages, the Chrome Web Store, page source, and files opened from your computer. The card can't exist there, so the side panel opens instead.

On an ordinary web page and still getting the panel? Check **Settings → Open as** — it may be set to **Docked side panel**.

## The card is in my way

Three things you can do, in increasing order of permanence:

- **Move or resize it** — drag its top bar to move it, or any edge or corner to resize. It remembers where you put it.
- **Snap it smaller** — the three size buttons in its header: **Dock to the side**, **Medium size**, **Fill the window**.
- **Switch to the docked panel for good** — **Settings → Open as → Docked side panel**. From then on the toolbar button opens the panel and the page makes room for it.

To close the card, click the toolbar button again, or the **×** in its header.

## A keyboard shortcut does nothing

Almost always another extension or app already owns that combination.

1. Open **Settings → Advanced → Keyboard shortcuts**. It shows what each command is bound to **right now**. If a command reads *not set*, it has no key.
2. Click **Change shortcuts…** (or go to `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`), and give it a different combination.
3. Come back — the list will already show the new key.

**Next workspace** and **Previous workspace** ship with no keys at all, on purpose. They do nothing until you give them some.

## I dragged a tab and nothing happened

Check what you dropped it on:

- **A folder.** Folders hold workspaces, not tabs. Hold still over the folder for a moment and it opens, then drop on a workspace inside it.
- **An archived workspace.** Archived workspaces don't take drops. Restore it first.
- **Empty space.** Only workspace rows, pinned workspace icons and the Resources pane accept a tab.

And two kinds of tab that can never be filed: **incognito tabs** (you'll see *"Incognito tabs can't be moved to a workspace."*) and Chrome's own internal pages.

## I dropped a tab in the wrong place

For **ten seconds** after any drop, the message in the corner has an **Undo** button. Click it, or press **⌘Z** (**Ctrl+Z** on Windows and Linux).

If ⌘Z did nothing:

- The ten seconds were up — the message has to still be on screen.
- Your cursor was in a text box. ⌘Z belongs to the text you're typing; click somewhere else first.

See [Undo](/help/unscrambled-tabs/moving-tabs#undo).

## My tab moved when I wanted a copy (or the other way round)

Dragging **moves** by default. Hold **⌥ Option** (**Alt** on Windows and Linux) *as you let go* to copy instead. The card that follows your cursor spells out which one you're about to get, so glance at it before you release.

If you'd rather it copied every time, turn on **Settings → Open Tabs view → Keep the original tab open when dragging to a workspace** — the modifier then moves instead.

## A workspace has vanished

Two likely explanations:

- **It was archived.** Scroll to the bottom of the workspace list for a collapsed **Archived (n)** section — click it open and use **Restore**.
- **You're searching for it.** Archived workspaces are deliberately kept out of search results. Open the Archived section instead.

If it isn't there either, it was deleted. Deletion has no undo, but if you have an [encrypted backup](/help/unscrambled-tabs/backup-import) from before, restoring it brings everything back.

## Right-clicking a workspace row gives me the browser's menu

That happens in two situations, both on purpose:

- You're **typing a new name** into that row — you may well want the browser's own text menu there.
- You're **ticking rows with checkboxes** — in selection mode a row has no menu of its own.

Finish or cancel (press **Esc**) and right-click will bring up the row's menu again. So will the **⋯** button that appears when you hover the row.

## A tab reloaded when I clicked it

It had been unloaded to free memory — the tab stays on your tab strip, but its page contents were released, so Chrome fetches it again when you come back.

- To stop it happening to a particular site, add that site to **Settings → Advanced → Protected Domains**.
- To stop it happening automatically at all, set **Settings → Advanced → Performance & RAM → Performance mode** to **Manual**.

Full detail on [Performance & RAM](/help/unscrambled-tabs/performance-and-ram).

## "Nothing to suspend here"

Everything in that window is protected: your active tab, pinned tabs, tabs playing sound, Chrome's own pages, and anything on your **Protected Domains** list are all left alone. That message means there was nothing left that was safe to unload.

## My snapshots list is empty

- **Session Time-Travel may be off.** **Settings → Advanced → Sessions** — the switch is at the top.
- **It may just be early.** The first snapshot appears after five minutes; the screen says so while it waits.

## An import file wasn't recognised

You'll see *"Unrecognized file format."* The Import box takes two kinds of file: an **Unscrambled Tabs encrypted backup** (the `.json` file the Export button produces) or a workspace export from the tool you're migrating from. Anything else is refused, and nothing is touched.

If you're restoring a backup and get *"Incorrect password"*, the password is the one you typed when you exported it. It can't be recovered or reset — that's the point of the encryption.

## The AI won't let me in

The assistant needs you signed in to eggz. The AI tab shows a **Sign in to eggz** card when you're not. If you sign in and the panel doesn't update, give it a few seconds, or close and reopen it. More on [Connect your eggz account](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account#troubleshooting).
