It is Tuesday, you have four jobs on the go, and thirty tabs open. Nine of them belong to Birdsong Pottery. Six belong to Marchmont Cafe. The rest you cannot account for, and closing any of them feels risky, so you keep the lot and the whole browser gets slower.

Unscrambled Tabs is the Chrome extension that sorts that out. In this part of the Help Centre you will learn to save a set of tabs under a name, switch between those sets, and file a stray tab where it belongs.

## What is Unscrambled Tabs?

Unscrambled Tabs is the Chrome extension half of eggz. [eggz](https://app.eggz.ai) is the web app you sign in to for your books. Unscrambled Tabs lives inside Chrome and looks after your browser instead: saved workspaces you can switch between, a place to file stray tabs, and controls for the memory that a hundred open tabs eats.

Click the button in your toolbar and it opens over the page you are reading. [Connect your eggz account](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account) and you also get the AI assistant (there is no key to paste), document upload and [bill capture](/help/unscrambled-tabs/capture-bills).

<Note>
eggz and Unscrambled Tabs share branding and product vision, but they are different surfaces. The web app is for accounting workflows. The extension is for tab and workspace management in Chrome. This section of the Help Centre covers the extension only.
</Note>

## Who it is for

If you keep many tabs open across many projects, this gives you saved workspaces to switch between, and a way to file a stray tab in one drag rather than bookmarking it and forgetting it.

If you are the sort of person who has already lost a session once, you also get snapshots to rewind to, duplicate clean-up, and memory controls that keep your tabs on the strip while freeing the RAM behind them.

## At a glance

| Area | What you get |
|------|----------------|
| Two ways to open it | The toolbar button opens either a floating card over the page (nothing on the page moves) or Chrome's docked side panel. **Settings → Open as** picks which. |
| Workspaces | Named workspaces with colours, icons, folders, pinning and archiving, one per Chrome window. Switching brings everything back: tab groups, pinned tabs, custom tab names, and the tab you were on. |
| Moving tabs | Drag a tab onto a workspace to file it. It moves by default; hold **⌥ Option** (**Alt**) to copy. A label follows your cursor saying which. Every drop has a ten-second **Undo**. |
| Right-click a row | Right-clicking any workspace row opens the same menu as its **⋯** button, right where your cursor is. |
| The command bar | On the dashboard, **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K**), or just **/**, finds your workspaces, tabs and links, and runs actions by name: create, switch, archive, suspend, close duplicates. |
| Tab tools | Rename any tab with your own name, save a page for later, close duplicates in one click, and suspend hungry tabs. |
| Open Tabs view | Every open tab across every window in one list. Jump, rename, suspend, save one tab or a whole window. |
| AI assistant | One chat that knows your open tabs and your saved workspaces, and, once you sign in, your eggz books. It is included with your eggz plan and there is no key to paste. |
| Capture bills | Spot a bill or invoice on any page and send it to eggz in one click, once you are signed in. |
| Search | **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+Shift+K** (Windows) from any tab, across workspaces, open tabs, saved tabs and saved links. |
| Quick switcher | **⌥A** (**Alt+A**) lists workspaces most-recent-first, and Enter flips you back to your previous one. |
| Session Time-Travel | Automatic five-minute snapshots, the last 50 kept. Restore any point as a fresh workspace. |
| Duplicate catcher | Open a page you already have and a bar offers to jump to the existing tab. |
| Performance modes | Unload eligible inactive tabs while keeping them on the tab strip: Manual, Balanced or Aggressive. |
| Encrypted backup | Export password-protected backups. Restoring one replaces local extension data. |

## How it relates to eggz

eggz is the web app: bookkeeping, receipts, customers. Sign in at [app.eggz.ai](https://app.eggz.ai).

Unscrambled Tabs is the extension: browser productivity, [installed from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/unscrambled-tabs/gfjdjhpcemmakhoaggnihfcohkmfcfcc).

They are designed to work together, but you do not need an eggz account for the extension's workspace and tab features.

## Official Chrome API reference

Tab unloading is built on Chrome's [`tabs.discard`](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/reference/api/tabs) API. Unloaded tabs stay visible, and when you activate one, Chrome reloads the page like a normal refresh.
