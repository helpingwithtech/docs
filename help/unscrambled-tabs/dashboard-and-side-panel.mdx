## One tool, two doors

Clicking the **Unscrambled Tabs** button in your toolbar opens the same thing either of two ways. You pick which.

- **A floating card** over the page you're reading (this is what you get out of the box). It sits on top of the page, so nothing on the page moves, reflows or resizes. You can drag it anywhere and resize it.
- **A docked side panel**, using Chrome's own panel at the edge of the window. The page makes room for it, and it stays open as you move from tab to tab.

Both show exactly the same thing: your open tabs, your workspaces, the AI assistant, search and settings.

### Choosing which one the button opens

Open **Settings** (the gear at the top of the card or panel) and the second section is **Open as**:

| Choice | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Floating card over the page** | The card floats on top of the page you're reading, so nothing on the page moves. Drag it anywhere, or resize it. |
| **Docked side panel** | The panel docks to the side of the window and the page makes room for it. It stays open as you move between tabs. |

The change takes effect on your very next click of the toolbar button — nothing to reload.

<Note>
Chrome doesn't let extensions draw on some pages — its own `chrome://` settings pages, the Web Store, page source, and local files. On those, the button opens the **side panel** instead, whichever way you've set **Open as**.
</Note>

## The floating card

Click the button and the card appears at the right-hand edge of the page, roughly the width of a side panel and the full height of the window less a small margin.

Along its top edge, from left to right:

- A **grip** of dots at the very left. Drag the top bar — anywhere that isn't a button — to move the card, exactly like a window's title bar.
- **Light / dark mode**, **Open Dashboard**, **Approvals** and **Settings** — the same four buttons the side panel has.
- **Dock to the side**, **Medium size** and **Fill the window** — three sizes to snap between. The one you're currently in is highlighted.
- **Move to the side panel** — retires the card and opens the docked panel instead, just for now. (Your **Open as** setting is unchanged, so the next click of the toolbar button still gives you a card.)
- **Close**.

You can also drag any **edge or corner** of the card to resize it freely. The card remembers where you put it and what size you made it — separately for each screen size, so a place you chose on a big external monitor isn't imposed on your laptop screen later.

Click the toolbar button again to close the card.

## The docked side panel

Chrome's own panel, at the edge of the window. From top to bottom:

**The header** carries four small buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Light / dark mode** | Flips the theme |
| **Open Dashboard** | Opens the full-page dashboard (in a pinned tab, so it's always where you left it) |
| **Approvals** | Your [automation approvals inbox](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search#the-approvals-inbox) — a badge shows how many decisions are waiting |
| **Settings** | Everything configurable, in one place — see [Settings](/help/unscrambled-tabs/settings-privacy) |

**Underneath, three tabs** — **Workspaces**, **AI Assistant** and **Search**:

- **Workspaces** is home: your [Open Tabs list](/help/unscrambled-tabs/open-tabs) at the top, then your workspaces, your folders, and a collapsed **Archived** section at the bottom if you have any. **New** beside the *Workspaces* heading creates a workspace; hover that heading and a folder icon appears beside it for a new folder.
- **AI Assistant** is [the chat](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search).
- **Search** finds workspaces, open tabs, saved tabs and saved links as you type.

(If you've set Open Tabs to **Dedicated tab** in Settings, an **Open Tabs** tab joins the row too.)

## The full-page dashboard

Click **Open Dashboard** in the header for the big version — a whole browser tab, for when it's tidy-up time rather than driving time. It shows the same data as the card and panel; a change in one appears in the other straight away.

### The sidebar — slim or open

The sidebar starts **slim**: a narrow column of labelled icons. An **Open sidebar** button at the very top, then **Open Tabs** carrying a badge with how many tabs you have open right now, then your **pinned workspaces**, then an icon per **folder** — hover one and its workspaces fan out beside it. **New workspace** sits at the foot.

Click **Open sidebar** — or press **⌘B** (**Ctrl+B** on Windows and Linux) — and it widens into the full list: **Open Tabs**, your **Active** workspace, a **Pinned** section, every folder with its workspaces, and **Archived** at the bottom, with **New Workspace** still at the foot. Press it again to go back to the slim column.

Archived workspaces never appear on the slim column or its flyouts — they live in the wide sidebar only.

The wide sidebar is where [dragging, reordering and bulk actions](/help/unscrambled-tabs/workspaces#select-several-at-once-bulk-actions) happen.

### The main area

Pick **Open Tabs** and the main area becomes the full [Open Tabs view](/help/unscrambled-tabs/open-tabs). Pick a workspace and you get two panes side by side:

- **Tabs** — the workspace's saved tabs ([click, rename, reorder, remove](/help/unscrambled-tabs/workspaces#the-tabs-inside-a-workspace)).
- **Resources** — its [saved links](/help/unscrambled-tabs/workspaces#resources-saved-links).

Drag the divider between them to resize the split; the dashboard remembers where you put it. Above the panes, the workspace's name, a **Search** button (or just press **/**), the theme toggle, and — when you're looking at a workspace this window isn't using — a **Switch to this workspace** button.

### The ask bar

Along the bottom of the dashboard sits a slim bar reading **"How can I help you today?"**. Click it, or press **⌘I** (**Ctrl+I** on Windows and Linux), and a row of suggestions comes up with it — *Summarize my open tabs*, *Capture a bill from this page*, *Organize tabs*. Type and press Enter, or tap a suggestion, and the full assistant expands over the page. **Esc** closes the chat; a second **Esc** tucks the bar back down to a line.

It's the same one chat as the side panel. See [AI assistant & the command bar](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search).

## Which should I use?

Honestly: all three. The card or the panel for driving — switching, saving, asking, all day. The dashboard when it's time to organise properly. A common rhythm is dashboard on Monday morning to get the week into workspaces, then the card the rest of the week to live in them.
