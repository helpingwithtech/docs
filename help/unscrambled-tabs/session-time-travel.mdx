## Your session, quietly photographed

**Session Time-Travel** takes an automatic snapshot of your current window's tabs **every 5 minutes** — what's open, the tab order, your Chrome tab groups, and which tab you were on. It keeps the most recent **50** snapshots, so you can rewind roughly the **last four hours** of browsing.

Closed the wrong ten tabs? Browser went down mid-research? A five-minute-old copy of your session is waiting.

It's on by default. The toggle lives in **Settings → Smart Features → Session Time-Travel**.

<Note>
Snapshots capture the window you were last working in — if you run several windows, it's the one in focus that gets photographed.
</Note>

## Restore a snapshot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Session Snapshots">
    Open the side panel, click the **Settings** gear, and scroll to **Session Snapshots**. The most recent snapshots are listed with how long ago they were taken and how many tabs each holds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Restore">
    Pick the moment you want back and click **Restore**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A new workspace appears">
    The snapshot is restored **as a brand-new workspace** — named after its time, like **Snapshot — Jul 30, 2:15 PM** — and the panel takes you to it. Your current tabs are **not** touched: nothing closes, nothing reloads. Switch to the snapshot workspace when you're ready, or just pluck the tabs you needed and delete it after.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Because a restore is just a workspace, everything on [Workspaces & tabs](/help/unscrambled-tabs/workspaces) applies — rename it, keep it, or delete it once it's served its purpose.
</Tip>

## When Chrome restarts

You don't need to do anything after a restart — Unscrambled Tabs picks your session back up on its own:

- If Chrome's own "continue where you left off" already brought your tabs back, the extension simply reconnects them to your active workspace — including your [custom tab names](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#rename-any-tab).
- If your tabs *didn't* come back, the extension **re-opens your active workspace** for you: tabs, pinned state, tab groups (names, colours, collapsed state), custom names, and the tab you were focused on.

Either way, you start the day where you ended the last one.

## Privacy & storage

Snapshots contain tab **titles and web addresses** and live in your browser's extension storage on your computer. They're included when you [export an encrypted backup](/help/unscrambled-tabs/backup-import), so a backup can carry your recent history to a new machine too.
