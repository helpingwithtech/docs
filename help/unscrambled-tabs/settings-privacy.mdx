## Where Settings lives

Click the **gear** at the top of the card or side panel. (The dashboard has no settings screen of its own — just a theme toggle.)

Settings is **two levels deep, no more**:

- **The main list** — the handful of things people actually change.
- **Advanced** — one row near the bottom that opens a page of the rarer stuff.
- Inside Advanced, **Sessions** opens one more page, because it holds a list that can run to fifty entries.

Every one of those pages carries a **back button at the top left, and it names where it goes**: Advanced's says **Settings**; Sessions' says **Advanced**. Coming back from Advanced puts you at the row you left, not at the top of the page.

## The main list

Top to bottom.

### eggz account

Whether you're connected to your eggz account, with **Sign in** / **Sign out**. One login is shared with the web app — the full story is on [Connect your eggz account](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account).

### Open as

What the toolbar button opens: **Floating card over the page** (the default) or **Docked side panel**. The change applies to your very next click. See [Two ways to open it](/help/unscrambled-tabs/dashboard-and-side-panel).

### Tabs

What the extension does as you open and close tabs.

- **Auto-save tabs** (on by default) — the workspace your window is using quietly keeps up with what you open and close, plus changes to tab groups and which tab you're on, so switching away never loses anything. Turn it off and workspaces only change when you act on them directly.
- **Tab deduplication** (on by default) — when you open a page you already have open, a small ["Already open" bar](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#already-open-the-duplicate-catcher) offers to switch you to the existing tab.

### Open Tabs view

Four controls that shape the [Open Tabs list](/help/unscrambled-tabs/open-tabs) everywhere.

| Setting | Options | Default |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Placement** | **Pinned section** — always visible above your workspaces · **Dedicated tab** — its own "Open Tabs" tab | Pinned section |
| **Sort tabs by** | **Window order (tab strip)** · **Most recently active** · **Site name (A–Z)** | Window order |
| **Group by window** | On — a divider per Chrome window · Off — one flat list | On |
| **Keep the original tab open when dragging to a workspace** | Off — the tab moves (it closes here) · On — it's copied and stays open | Off |

That last one changes the default for [dragging an open tab](/help/unscrambled-tabs/moving-tabs). Holding **⌥ Option** (**Alt** on Windows and Linux) as you let go always does the opposite of whatever you've set, and the card that follows your cursor names which one you're getting.

### Default Favicon Emoji

When a site's own icon can't be loaded, this emoji stands in for it in your lists. Click the emoji to pick a different one (the default is 🌐).

### Import & Export

**Export encrypted backup** and the **Import** dropzone — covered in depth on [Backup & import](/help/unscrambled-tabs/backup-import).

### Advanced

A row with a chevron, not a toggle. It opens the Advanced page — see below.

### About

The extension name, the version you're running, and where the AI comes from. Handy when you're talking to support.

### Danger Zone

**Wipe all data** permanently deletes all workspaces, folders, resources and settings from this browser, and disconnects your eggz account here. It asks you to confirm — and it means it.

<Warning>
There is no undo for **Wipe all data**. If there's any chance you'll want your setup back, [export an encrypted backup](/help/unscrambled-tabs/backup-import) first.
</Warning>

## Advanced

Four groups, and nothing else. Three of them are right there on the page; the fourth is a door.

### Performance & RAM

- **Performance mode** — **Manual** (no automatic unloading), **Balanced** (unload eligible inactive tabs after your chosen interval), or **Aggressive** (same, with the idle threshold capped at five minutes). Default: Manual.
- **Discard after** — the Balanced/Aggressive idle interval: 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes (default 15). It appears once you've picked something other than Manual.
- **Free RAM now** — a one-click sweep that unloads eligible inactive tabs immediately, whatever mode you're in. It tells you how many it did.

All of it skips your active tab, pinned tabs, tabs playing sound, Chrome's own pages, and every site on your Protected Domains list. The full picture is on [Performance & RAM](/help/unscrambled-tabs/performance-and-ram#performance-modes-explained).

### Protected Domains

Sites that are **never** unloaded — so drafts, forms and logged-in dashboards are always safe. Type one domain per line and click **Save**. You can list up to 100.

Anything here is skipped by performance-mode sweeps, by **Free RAM now**, and by the manual [Suspend buttons](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#suspend-a-tab). Good candidates: your webmail, your documents tool, your banking, your payroll or HR system — and anything you routinely leave a half-written form in.

<Tip>
Paste addresses however you like. A full web address is tidied down to just its domain when you save, so `https://www.example.com/inbox/2` becomes `example.com`.
</Tip>

### Keyboard shortcuts

A read-only list of every command and **the key it is bound to right now** — not the factory setting, but yours. Unbound commands read *not set*. **Change shortcuts…** opens Chrome's own shortcut page; come back and the list has already caught up. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/help/unscrambled-tabs/keyboard-shortcuts).

### Sessions

A row with a chevron and a count. It opens a page of its own holding both halves of [Session Time-Travel](/help/unscrambled-tabs/session-time-travel):

- The **Session Time-Travel** switch, with what it does written next to it: a snapshot of your open tabs every 5 minutes, and the last 50 kept.
- **Snapshots** — the whole list, newest first, each showing how long ago it was taken and how many tabs it holds, with a **Restore** button.

Its back button says **Advanced**, because that's where it came from.

## Light and dark

The theme toggle lives in the **header** of the card, the side panel and the dashboard (sun/moon), not in Settings. Both themes are designed for comfortable contrast.

## Your privacy, plainly

- **Your tabs, workspaces, snapshots and settings live locally in your browser.** Core tab and workspace features need no account and no internet.
- **The AI and eggz features are optional.** When you use them, only names and web addresses are shared by default — your open tabs' titles and addresses, and your workspace, folder and saved-link names. A page's **actual content** is sent only after you approve the permission card, each time. See [It asks before it reads a page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search#it-asks-before-it-reads-a-page).
- **Signing in, uploads and bill capture use your eggz account** and follow the web app's data handling. The extension never stores passwords — your session is the same secure browser cookie the web app uses.
- **The bill-capture nudge is local.** To offer the occasional ["capture any bills?" suggestion](/help/unscrambled-tabs/capture-bills#the-gentle-capture-any-bills-nudge), the extension keeps a short, rolling list of site names you've visited recently. It lives only in memory for the current browser session, is wiped when Chrome closes, and is never sent anywhere.
- **Search is local.** Finding your tabs and workspaces happens on your computer.

## About the permissions Chrome asks for

The extension needs broad **tab** access to save workspaces, switch tab sets, rename tabs, take snapshots and unload tabs — and its right-click menu items (**Rename this tab…**, **Save for later & close tab**, **Move tab to workspace**) need permission to appear on pages. It also needs to draw on the page you're reading, because that is what the floating card is. Granting only some permissions isn't supported — if you decline, some features won't work.
