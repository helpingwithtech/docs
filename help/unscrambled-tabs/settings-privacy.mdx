You want the panel docked instead of floating over the page, and you want your webmail left alone when the extension starts freeing memory. Both are in Settings, a few rows apart.

This page covers everything in there, in the order you meet it, and finishes with what happens to your data.

## Where Settings lives

Click the gear at the top of the card or side panel. The dashboard has no settings screen of its own, only a theme toggle.

Settings is two levels deep and no more. There is the main list, which holds the handful of things people actually change. Near the bottom, one **Advanced** row opens a page of the rarer stuff. Inside Advanced, **Sessions** opens one more page, because it holds a list that can run to fifty entries.

Every one of those pages carries a back button at the top left, and it names where it goes. Advanced's says **Settings**. Sessions' says **Advanced**. Coming back from Advanced puts you at the row you left rather than the top of the page.

## The main list

Top to bottom.

### eggz account

Whether you are connected to your eggz account, with **Sign in** and **Sign out**. One login is shared with the web app. The full story is on [Connect your eggz account](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account).

**Show the approvals inbox** sits here too, and it is **off**. While it is off the extension never asks eggz whether anything is waiting, so there is no tray icon and no background checking at all. Turn it on and the inbox appears in the header of the card and the side panel, with a badge counting the decisions your organisation's automations are holding for a person. See [The approvals inbox](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search#the-approvals-inbox).

**File bills you download** is a row here with a screen of its own, and it is **off**. While it is off nothing is watched at all: the extension registers no download listener and reads nothing. Turn it on and it watches downloads **from pages where eggz has found bills**, and files each one as it lands. Nothing else is watched. Each one files a document and uses one document credit, the same as a bill you upload by hand, and the screen keeps a list of what it has filed. It is the answer when a supplier's site will not hand a file to the extension but will hand it to you: [collect](/help/unscrambled-tabs/collect-bills) says so, and this catches them as you click the site's own download button.

### Open as

What the toolbar button opens: **Floating card over the page**, which is the default, or **Docked side panel**. The change applies to your very next click. See [Two ways to open it](/help/unscrambled-tabs/dashboard-and-side-panel).

### Tabs

What the extension does as you open and close tabs.

**Auto-save tabs**, on by default, means the workspace your window is using quietly keeps up with what you open and close, plus changes to tab groups and which tab you are on, so switching away never loses anything. Turn it off and workspaces only change when you act on them directly.

**Tab deduplication**, also on by default, means that when you open a page you already have open, a small ["Already open" bar](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#already-open-the-duplicate-catcher) offers to switch you to the existing tab.

**Keep the eggz tab pinned**, on by default, puts an eggz tab at the front of the tab strip in every ordinary window, so your workspaces are always one click away. Close it and it stays closed in that window for the rest of the session rather than springing back at you, and it returns next time you start Chrome. Turn the setting off and it is not created at all.

### Open Tabs view

Four controls that shape the [Open Tabs list](/help/unscrambled-tabs/open-tabs) everywhere.

| Setting | Options | Default |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Placement** | **Pinned section**, always visible above your workspaces · **Dedicated tab**, its own "Open Tabs" tab | Pinned section |
| **Sort tabs by** | **Window order (tab strip)** · **Most recently active** · **Site name (A–Z)** | Window order |
| **Group by window** | On, a divider per Chrome window · Off, one flat list | On |
| **Keep the original tab open when dragging to a workspace** | Off, the tab moves and closes here · On, it is copied and stays open | Off |

That last one changes the default for [dragging an open tab](/help/unscrambled-tabs/moving-tabs). Holding **⌥ Option** (**Alt** on Windows and Linux) as you let go always does the opposite of whatever you have set, and the card that follows your cursor names which one you are getting.

### Default Favicon Emoji

When a site's own icon cannot be loaded, this emoji stands in for it in your lists. Click the emoji to pick a different one. The default is 🌐.

### Import & Export

**Export encrypted backup** and the **Import** dropzone, covered in depth on [Backup & import](/help/unscrambled-tabs/backup-import).

### Advanced

A row with a chevron rather than a toggle. It opens the Advanced page, described below.

### About

The extension name, the version you are running, and where the AI comes from. Useful when you are talking to support.

### Danger Zone

**Wipe all data** permanently deletes all workspaces, folders, resources, session snapshots and settings from this browser, and disconnects your eggz account here. It asks you to confirm, and it means it. Snapshots go with everything else, which matters because they hold the name and address of every tab that was in them.

<Warning>
There is no undo for **Wipe all data**. If there is any chance you will want your setup back, [export an encrypted backup](/help/unscrambled-tabs/backup-import) first.
</Warning>

## Advanced

Four groups and nothing else. Three of them are right there on the page. The fourth opens a page of its own.

### Performance & RAM

**Performance mode** gives you **Manual**, which does no automatic unloading, **Balanced**, which unloads eligible inactive tabs after your chosen interval, or **Aggressive**, which does the same with the idle threshold capped at five minutes. The default is Manual.

**Discard after** is the Balanced and Aggressive idle interval: 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, with 15 as the default. It appears once you have picked something other than Manual.

**Free RAM now** is a one-click sweep that unloads eligible inactive tabs immediately, whatever mode you are in. It tells you how many it did.

All of it skips your active tab, pinned tabs, tabs playing sound, Chrome's own pages, and every site on your Protected Domains list. The full picture is on [Performance & RAM](/help/unscrambled-tabs/performance-and-ram#performance-modes-explained).

### Protected Domains

Sites that are never unloaded, so drafts, forms and logged-in dashboards are always safe. Type one domain per line and click **Save**. You can list up to 100.

Anything here is skipped by performance-mode sweeps, by **Free RAM now**, and by the manual [Suspend buttons](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#suspend-a-tab). Good candidates are your webmail, your documents tool, your banking, your payroll or HR system, and anything you routinely leave a half-written form in.

<Tip>
Paste addresses however you like. A full web address is tidied down to just its domain when you save, so `https://www.example.com/inbox/2` becomes `example.com`.
</Tip>

### Keyboard shortcuts

A read-only list of every command and the key it is bound to right now, which is yours rather than the factory setting. Unbound commands read *not set*. **Change shortcuts…** opens Chrome's own shortcut page, and when you come back the list has already caught up. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/help/unscrambled-tabs/keyboard-shortcuts).

### Sessions

A row with a chevron and a count. It opens a page of its own holding both halves of [Session Time-Travel](/help/unscrambled-tabs/session-time-travel).

At the top is the **Session Time-Travel** switch, with what it does written next to it: a snapshot of your open tabs every 5 minutes, and the last 50 kept. Underneath, **Snapshots** is the whole list, newest first, each showing how long ago it was taken and how many tabs it holds, with a **Restore** button.

Its back button says **Advanced**, because that is where it came from.

## Light and dark

The theme toggle lives in the header of the card, the side panel and the dashboard, as a sun or moon, rather than in Settings. Both themes are designed for comfortable contrast.

## Your privacy, plainly

Your tabs, workspaces, snapshots and settings live locally in your browser. Core tab and workspace features need no account and no internet.

The AI and eggz features are optional. When you use them, only names and web addresses are shared by default: your open tabs' titles and addresses, and your workspace, folder and saved-link names. A page's actual content is sent only after you approve the permission card, each time. See [It asks before it reads a page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search#it-asks-before-it-reads-a-page).

Signing in, uploads and bill capture use your eggz account and follow the web app's data handling. The extension never stores passwords. Your session is the same secure browser cookie the web app uses.

The bill-capture nudge is local. To offer the occasional ["capture any bills?" suggestion](/help/unscrambled-tabs/capture-bills#the-gentle-capture-any-bills-nudge), the extension keeps a short, rolling list of site names you have visited recently. It lives only in memory for the current browser session, is wiped when Chrome closes, and is never sent anywhere.

Search is local too. Finding your tabs and workspaces happens on your computer.

## About the permissions Chrome asks for

The extension needs broad tab access to save workspaces, switch tab sets, rename tabs, take snapshots and unload tabs. Its right-click menu items (**Rename this tab…**, **Save for later & close tab**, **Move tab to workspace**) need permission to appear on pages, and it needs to draw on the page you are reading, because that is what the floating card is.

Granting only some permissions is not supported. If you decline, some features will not work.
