## Where Settings lives

All settings are in the **side panel**: click the **Settings** gear in the panel header. (The dashboard doesn't have its own settings screen — just theme toggles.) Here's every section, top to bottom.

## Protected Domains

Sites that are **never suspended** — so drafts, forms, and logged-in dashboards are always safe from memory-saving. Type one domain per line (like `mail.google.com`) and click **Save**. You can list up to 100.

Anything here is skipped by [Performance mode](/help/unscrambled-tabs/performance-and-ram) sweeps, **Free RAM now**, and the manual [Suspend buttons](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#suspend-a-tab). Add your email, docs, banking, and anything with a half-written form.

## Default Favicon Emoji

When a site's own icon can't be loaded, this emoji stands in for it in your lists. Click the emoji to pick a different one (the default is 🌐).

## eggz account

Shows whether you're connected to your eggz account, with **Sign in** / **Sign out**. One login is shared with the web app — the full story is on [Connect your eggz account](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account).

## Import & Export

**Export encrypted backup** and the **Import** dropzone — covered in depth on [Backup & import](/help/unscrambled-tabs/backup-import).

## Auto-save tabs

On by default. As you browse, the workspace your window is using quietly keeps up — tabs you open and close, plus changes to tab groups and which tab you're on, are saved in the background so switching away never loses anything. Turn it off and workspaces only change when you act on them directly.

## Open Tabs view

Three controls that shape the [Open Tabs view](/help/unscrambled-tabs/open-tabs) everywhere:

| Setting | Options | Default |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Placement** | **Pinned section** — always visible above workspaces · **Dedicated tab** — its own "Open Tabs" view in the side panel | Pinned section |
| **Sort tabs by** | **Window order (tab strip)** · **Most recently active** · **Site name (A–Z)** | Window order |
| **Group by window** | On — a divider per Chrome window · Off — one flat list | On |

## Smart Features

- **Tab deduplication** (on by default) — when you open a page you already have open, a small ["Already open" bar](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#already-open-the-duplicate-catcher) offers to switch you to the existing tab.
- **Session Time-Travel** (on by default) — the automatic five-minute snapshots. See [Session Time-Travel](/help/unscrambled-tabs/session-time-travel).
- **Performance & RAM** — the automatic memory-saving controls:
  - **Performance mode** — **Manual** (no automatic suspending), **Balanced** (suspend eligible inactive tabs after your chosen interval), or **Aggressive** (same, with the idle threshold capped at five minutes). Default: Manual.
  - **Discard after** — the Balanced/Aggressive idle interval: 5, 10, 15, 30, or 60 minutes (default 15).
  - **Free RAM now** — a one-click sweep that suspends eligible inactive tabs immediately.

  All of it skips active, pinned, and audible tabs, internal pages, and your Protected Domains — the full picture is on [Performance & RAM](/help/unscrambled-tabs/performance-and-ram#performance-modes-explained).

## Session Snapshots

The list of your automatic snapshots — newest first, with a **Restore** button on each. Restoring creates a new workspace and touches nothing else; see [Session Time-Travel](/help/unscrambled-tabs/session-time-travel#restore-a-snapshot).

## About

The extension name and version — handy when you're talking to support.

## Danger Zone

**Wipe all data** permanently deletes all workspaces, folders, resources, and settings from this browser, and disconnects your eggz account here. It asks you to confirm (**Confirm wipe**) — and it means it. If there's any chance you'll want your setup back, [export an encrypted backup](/help/unscrambled-tabs/backup-import) first.

## Light and dark

The theme toggle lives in the side panel and dashboard **headers** (sun/moon), not in Settings. Both themes are designed for comfortable contrast.

## Your privacy, plainly

- **Your tabs, workspaces, snapshots, and settings live locally in your browser.** Core tab and workspace features need no account and no internet.
- **The AI and eggz features are optional.** When you use them, only names and web addresses are shared by default — your open tabs' titles and addresses, and your workspace, folder, and saved-link names. A page's **actual content** is sent only after you approve the permission card, each time. See [It asks before it reads a page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search#it-asks-before-it-reads-a-page).
- **Signing in, uploads, and bill capture use your eggz account** and follow the web app's data handling. The extension never stores passwords — your session is the same secure browser cookie the web app uses.
- **The bill-capture nudge is local.** To offer the occasional ["capture any bills?" suggestion](/help/unscrambled-tabs/capture-bills#the-gentle-capture-any-bills-nudge), the extension keeps a short, rolling list of site names you've visited recently. It lives only in memory for the current browser session, is wiped when Chrome closes, and is never sent anywhere.
- **Search is local.** Finding your tabs and workspaces happens on your computer.

## About the permissions Chrome asks for

The extension needs broad tab access to save workspaces, switch tab sets, rename tabs, take snapshots, and suspend tabs — and its right-click menu items (**Rename this tab…**, **Save for later & close tab**, **Move tab to workspace**) need permission to appear on pages. Granting only some permissions isn't supported — if you decline, some features won't work.
