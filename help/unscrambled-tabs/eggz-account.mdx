The tab and workspace side of eggz Tab Manager works without an account. Connecting your eggz account is what adds the AI assistant, document upload and bill capture.

This page shows you how to sign in, what signing in unlocks, and what to do when the panel says you are signed out.

## One login, both surfaces

You sign in once, either in the extension or in the [web app](https://app.eggz.ai), and both surfaces share the same session.

Sign in from the extension and you are also signed in at `app.eggz.ai`. If you are already signed in to the web app, the side panel detects your session automatically, with no extra clicks. Signing out on either surface signs you out of both.

Connecting an account is optional for the browser side of things. Workspaces, performance modes, snapshots and backup all work without it.

## Sign in

1. Open the side panel and choose **Sign in to eggz**, on the AI tab or in **Settings → eggz account**.
2. A browser tab opens with the eggz login. Sign in as usual.
3. The success page closes itself and the side panel shows your account within a few seconds.

<Note>
The extension never stores passwords or tokens. Your session lives in a secure browser cookie on `app.eggz.ai`, the same one the web app uses.
</Note>

### No organisation yet?

If your account has no organisation, which happens when you have only just signed up, finish onboarding in the web app first. See [Getting started](/help/guides/onboarding). Uploads and chat about your books need an organisation to belong to.

## Upload documents from the side panel

When you are signed in, the side panel can send bills, receipts and invoices straight into your eggz organisation.

**Upload document** takes a PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP file up to 10MB. **Send tab screenshot** captures the current tab, for example an invoice open in webmail, and uploads it as an image.

Uploads use the same document credits and extraction pipeline as the web app. The file appears in your dashboard like any other upload, and extraction starts automatically. See [Uploading documents](/help/guides/uploading-documents) for what happens next.

If your organisation has used all its document credits, the upload card shows an upgrade prompt with a link to **Billing**.

## The AI assistant is included

Signing in unlocks the AI assistant, with no API key to paste. There is one chat, with no modes to switch between, and it handles both your eggz books (bills, invoices, quotes, accounts, receipts) and your tabs and workspaces (chat, **Organize**, save-resources).

It is all covered by your eggz plan. See [AI assistant & the command bar](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search). Fair-use limits apply, and if your organisation is unusually heavy the assistant will ask you to upgrade. You can also [invite a friend](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search#when-you-run-low-on-ai-credits) for free credits.

## Shared credits

Document processing credits belong to your organisation, not to the surface you upload from. An upload from the extension consumes the same credits as an upload from the dashboard: one upload is one document credit, however many pages the file has. A document eggz already holds from the same website is not charged again when the extension collects it; see [How document credits are counted](/help/guides/uploading-documents#how-document-credits-are-counted). See your plan and live usage in **Settings → Billing** in the web app.

## Sign out

Use **Settings → eggz account → Sign out** in the side panel, or sign out of the web app. Either one ends the shared session everywhere.

## Troubleshooting

If you see **"Your eggz session has expired"**, sign in again from the side panel. Sessions end after periods of inactivity, or when you sign out elsewhere.

If the sign-in tab opened but the panel did not update, the extension also checks your session in the background. Give it a few seconds, or close and reopen the side panel.

If cookies are blocked, the shared login cannot work, because it relies on the browser cookie for `app.eggz.ai`. If you block all cookies for that site in Chrome settings (`chrome://settings/cookies`), the extension cannot see your session, so allow cookies for `app.eggz.ai`.

If you are stuck signed out on a work profile, some managed Chrome profiles restrict extension network access. Try the same flow in a personal profile to confirm.
