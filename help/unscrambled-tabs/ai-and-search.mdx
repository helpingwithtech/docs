## One chat

Open the **AI Assistant** tab and just type. There are no modes to switch between: one chat handles your bills, invoices, quotes, accounts and receipts *and* your open tabs and workspaces.

The assistant is **included** with your eggz plan — no API key to paste, no secret codes, no surprise bills.

<Note>
Sign in to unlock the AI — the easiest path is to [connect your eggz account](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account). Signed out, the AI tab shows a **Sign in to eggz** card instead. Once you're in, the AI is covered by your eggz plan (fair use applies).
</Note>

Two nice touches: replies **stream in live** as they're written, so you're never staring at a blank box; and your conversation is kept with your account, so you can **close the browser, come back later, and it's still there**.

## It knows what you've got open (the helpful way)

Here's the fun part. The chat doesn't just see the one tab you're staring at. It knows about **every open tab in every window** — even the sleepy ones that Chrome has put down for a nap to save memory. By default it only sees the **names and web addresses** (the titles and URLs), so it knows what you've got going on without poking each tab awake.

It also knows the **names** of things in your **saved library** — your workspaces, folders, and saved links. So you can ask things like:

- *"Which of my open tabs are about the kitchen reno?"*
- *"Did I already save a workspace for the tax stuff?"*
- *"Round up all my shopping tabs and tuck them into one group."*

<Tip>
Sleeping tabs stay asleep. The chat sees the name and address of a napping tab, but it won't wake it up just to peek — that would waste the very memory you were trying to save.
</Tip>

## It asks before it reads a page {#it-asks-before-it-reads-a-page}

Names and addresses are one thing — actually reading **what's written on a page** is another. So the chat **asks first**. When it wants to read the contents of a page (or look at one of your records), a little permission card pops up — it even flags how sensitive the request is — and you pick:

- **Approve** — read this page, just for this request.
- **Deny** — nope, hands off.

It asks **every time** it wants to read a page, so you're never handing over a blanket pass. The permission card appears right in the chat, at the moment it's needed.

### The Approvals inbox

Separate from page-read permission, the header of the card and side panel has an **Approvals** inbox (the tray icon, with a badge showing the count). This is where your organisation's **eggz automations** queue anything that wants a human decision — like a drafted bill waiting for a yes — so nothing automated ever slips through without you. It's there when you're signed in with your organisation; approve or decline each card and it's on its way.

<Note>
**Nothing on a page leaves your browser until you say yes.** Until you approve, the chat only ever sees names and web addresses — never the actual words on the page.
</Note>

When you do say yes, the extension reads the page **cleanly** — it pulls out the real article or content and skips the menus, ads, and clutter — so the AI gets the good stuff and not the junk. And when the assistant writes back, any images in its reply are shown as plain text rather than loaded, so a dodgy page can't use them to phone home.

## Organise your tabs with a nudge {#organise-your-tabs-with-a-nudge}

There's an **Organize** button in the chat header. Give it a tap and the AI sorts your open tabs into sensible groups — across **all** your windows, not just the one in front of you. You can then drop those groups straight into a **new or existing workspace** so they're saved for later. On the dashboard, the same thing is one of the suggestions on the ask bar.

## "Hey, want to capture that bill?"

If you've been browsing supplier or shop websites, the chat may pop up a little suggestion chip — something like *"You've visited supplier.com today — capture any bills?"* Tap it and your question is already typed out for you.

It only does this after you've visited a couple of different sites recently, so it's a gentle nudge, not a nag. Learn more on [Capture bills from any page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/capture-bills).

If your eggz plan's included AI gets used up, the chat doesn't just slam the door — you'll see a friendly **upgrade card**. Bonus: it also lets you **invite a friend**. When they join, **you both get free AI credits**. Sharing is caring (and also free credits).

## Search anything {#search-anything-k}

Need to find a workspace, a tab, or a saved link in a hurry? Pop open search from your keyboard — from *any* tab:

- **Mac:** ⌘K
- **Windows / Linux:** Ctrl+Shift+K

Start typing and matches surface instantly, grouped into four buckets:

- **Workspaces** — press **Enter** and you switch to it.
- **Open tabs** — every live tab across every window, freshest first. **Enter** jumps you straight to that tab, wherever it is.
- **Saved tabs** — tabs stored inside your workspaces, including any [custom names you've given them](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#rename-any-tab).
- **Resources** — your saved links, with the section they live in.

Use ↑/↓ to move, **Enter** to go, **Esc** to close. Archived workspaces stay out of the results on purpose — search is for what you're working on.

Search runs over your own saved data on your computer — it doesn't need the internet or your account.

## The command bar {#the-command-bar}

On the **dashboard**, the same box does something more: as well as finding things, it **runs actions by name**.

Open it while the dashboard is the tab you're on:

- Press **/** (the quickest)
- Press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux)
- Click the **Search** button in the dashboard header

(Pressing the global search shortcut while you're on the dashboard opens this rather than the side panel.)

Type nothing and you get a list of things you can do; type a word and it narrows. **Commands** always sit at the top, above the four search buckets.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Create workspace** | Names one and captures this window |
| **New folder** | Adds a folder to the sidebar |
| **Switch workspace…** | Closes this set of tabs and opens another |
| **Save this tab to…** | Keeps the page you were on, leaves it open |
| **Move tab to workspace…** | Saves the page and closes it here |
| **Archive workspace…** | Keeps everything, takes it out of the lists |
| **Suspend other tabs** | Frees their memory; they reload when clicked |
| **Close duplicate tabs** | Same page open twice — keeps one |
| **Toggle sidebar** | Shows or hides the workspace list |
| **Open settings** | Account, backup, shortcuts and more |

A name ending in **…** asks a second question — pick **Switch workspace…** and it follows up with your workspace list. **Esc** steps back.

A command only appears when it would actually do something: **Close duplicate tabs** stays out of sight unless you really have a duplicate, and the tab commands need a web page to act on. Six are shown at a time, so keep typing to reach the rest.

<Tip>
It matches on more than the exact words. *dup* finds **Close duplicate tabs**, *susp* finds **Suspend other tabs**, and initials work too — **cw** finds **Create workspace**.
</Tip>

## Jump to the AI assistant

Want the chat right now?

- **Mac:** ⌘J
- **Windows / Linux:** Ctrl+Shift+J

On the dashboard, **⌘I** (**Ctrl+I**) opens the ask bar along the bottom instead — same chat, without leaving the page.

## Not into AI? No problem

You can skip the AI entirely and the rest of the extension hums along just fine. **Workspaces**, **Performance modes**, **snapshots**, and **backup** all work with zero AI. The AI only taps you on the shoulder for AI-specific things.
