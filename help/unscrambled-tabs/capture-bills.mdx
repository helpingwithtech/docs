## Catch bills in the wild

Bills are sneaky. They hide in your email, on supplier websites, and inside PDFs you open and forget about. The eggz Chrome extension helps you grab them the moment you see them, before they turn into an "I'll deal with it later" pile.

There are two shapes to this. **One bill on the page you are reading** is the banner below. **A page that lists dozens of them**, like a supplier's billing portal, is one approval and a single run: see [Collect every bill on a page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/collect-bills).

<Note>
This feature needs you to be [signed in to eggz](/help/unscrambled-tabs/eggz-account), since that's where your captured bills go.
</Note>

## The "looks like a bill" banner

When you land on a page that looks like an invoice or a bill — including PDFs opened right in Chrome — a little banner slides in at the top of the side panel and offers to save it for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a bill or invoice">
    Visit a supplier's invoice page, or open a bill PDF in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spot the banner">
    A banner pops up offering to **capture** the page. It stays until you close it with the **✕**. It will not time out and vanish while you are still reading the very page it is about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to eggz">
    Click to capture. The extension grabs the **original invoice** — the actual PDF, filename and all pages intact — and sends it straight into your eggz organisation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The capture uses the **same upload pipeline and credits** as the dashboard, so your bill lands in [Expenses](/help/guides/expenses) and starts being read automatically — supplier, amounts, tax and all. See [Uploading documents](/help/guides/uploading-documents) for what happens after.

<Tip>
Switched tabs and the banner vanished? No drama. It belongs to the tab it found the bill on, so it never follows you around. Go back to the bill page and it will offer again.
</Tip>

## The gentle "capture any bills?" nudge

The [AI assistant](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search) also keeps a light touch on this. If you've been hopping around a few different supplier or shop websites, the chat may show a suggestion chip like *"You've visited supplier.com today — capture any bills?"* Tap it and your message is pre-filled, ready to go.

It only shows up after you've visited a couple of sites recently, so it's a friendly heads-up, not a pushy salesperson.

## What gets sent (and what doesn't)

Keeping it honest:

- When the bill is a **PDF** — including one opened in Chrome's built-in viewer — the capture sends the **original PDF file**, filename and all pages intact, so multi-page invoices arrive complete. Only an on-screen **web-page** invoice falls back to a picture of what's visible.
- It sends anything at all **only when you click to capture**.
- The "looks like a bill" check happens **on your computer**. Pages aren't sent anywhere just for being looked at.
- Your everyday browsing is **not** uploaded. Nothing leaves your browser until you choose to capture.

<Warning>
The bill-detector is smart, but not psychic. It may occasionally wave at a page that isn't really a bill — close it with the **✕** and it will not raise that page again. And when a web page is captured as a picture (rather than an original PDF), glance over the screen first so nothing private tags along.
</Warning>

## What a capture costs

- One capture is one document and uses **one document credit**, however many pages the PDF has. Capturing the same bill twice processes it twice and uses two credits.
- The one case eggz does not charge again is when a [collect](/help/unscrambled-tabs/collect-bills) fetches the invoices or receipts listed on a page for you and eggz already holds that document from the same website. See [How document credits are counted](/help/guides/uploading-documents#how-document-credits-are-counted).

## If your credits run out

Bills are captured with the same **document credits** as the rest of eggz. If your organisation runs out, the capture card shows an upgrade prompt with a link to **Billing**. Upgrade and you're back in business.

## A whole page of bills at once

A supplier's billing portal listing a year of invoices does not need a hundred clicks. When the extension finds three or more documents on one page it offers to fetch them all, tells you how many are new before you decide, and lets you narrow the run to invoices only, receipts only, or a few you tick by hand.

See [Collect every bill on a page](/help/unscrambled-tabs/collect-bills).
