## What a workspace is

A **workspace** is a **saved set of browser tabs** with a name, a colour, and an icon — one workspace per client, project, or hat you wear. Switch to a workspace and your window becomes that project's tabs; switch away and everything is saved, exactly as you left it, ready for next time.

## Create a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New">
    In the **side panel**, click **New** at the top of the Workspaces list. On the **dashboard**, click **New Workspace** at the bottom of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and make it yours">
    Give it a name (up to 40 characters — "Acme Pty Ltd", "Tax time", "Weekend reno"), pick an **icon** and a **colour**. A live preview shows how it'll look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Workspace">
    Done — and here's the nice part: **the tabs you have open right now are added automatically**, so your current session is instantly safe. (It captures the window you created it from.)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Other ways to spawn a workspace: **Save all** on a window in the [Open Tabs view](/help/unscrambled-tabs/open-tabs#whole-window-moves) turns a whole window into a workspace, and restoring a [session snapshot](/help/unscrambled-tabs/session-time-travel) creates one too.
</Tip>

## Switching — everything comes back {#switching}

Switching is the heart of the product, so here's exactly what happens. When you switch this window from workspace A to workspace B:

1. **A is saved first** — its tabs, their order, which tabs were pinned, your Chrome **tab groups** (names, colours, and whether they were collapsed), **which tab you were focused on**, and any [custom tab names](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#rename-any-tab).
2. **B opens in the same window** — and not just its tabs: its **tab groups are rebuilt**, **pinned tabs are pinned again** (including the first one), your **custom tab names are back on the strip**, and the **exact tab you were on last time is focused**.

No mystery windows, no "roughly what you had". You leave a workspace mid-thought and come back to the same thought.

**Where to click:**

- **Side panel** — click the workspace's name (or the ▶ **Switch to workspace** button that appears on hover).
- **Dashboard sidebar** — clicking a row *shows* that workspace's tabs and links so you can peek without committing; to actually switch, use the **Switch** pill that appears when you hover the row, or the **Switch to this workspace** button in the header.
- **Keyboard** — **Alt+A** opens the quick switcher: most-recent first, and **Enter flips you straight back to your previous workspace**. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/help/unscrambled-tabs/keyboard-shortcuts).

### Next / previous workspace

Two more commands — **Switch to the next workspace** and **Switch to the previous workspace** — step through your workspace list in order, wrapping around at the end. They ship without keys; give them keys at `chrome://extensions/shortcuts` and you can cycle workspaces without ever opening a panel.

## One workspace per window

Each Chrome window remembers **its own** workspace. Switching only ever affects the window you're in, so you can keep Client A on your left monitor and Client B on your right, each window's side panel and dashboard showing its own world.

Want that second window without leaving your first? Hover a workspace row on the dashboard and click **Open in new window** — the workspace opens in a fresh Chrome window (groups, pins and all) and that window is now bound to it. Your current window doesn't change. The same button lives in the pinned-workspace flyouts on the sidebar rail.

## Personalise a workspace

Hover a workspace row and click **⋯** for the menu. What's available:

| Action | Side panel | Dashboard | What it does |
|--------|:---:|:---:|--------------|
| **Rename** | ✓ | ✓ | Type the new name (inline in the panel; a small dialog on the dashboard) |
| **Pin to sidebar** / **Unpin from sidebar** | ✓ | ✓ | Pinned workspaces get their own **Pinned** section — and their own icons at the top of the dashboard's sidebar rail, one click away all day |
| **Change colour** | — | ✓ | Pick from the nine workspace colours |
| **Set emoji icon** | — | ✓ | Opens a searchable emoji picker — the emoji replaces the row's icon everywhere. **Reset to favicon** puts it back |
| **Duplicate** | — | ✓ | Makes "*name* (copy)" right below the original — tabs, tab groups, and saved links all copied. Great for a template workspace you clone per client |
| **Move to folder** | — | ✓ | File the workspace into any folder (or back to **No folder**) |
| **Delete** | ✓ | ✓ | Asks first — "This cannot be undone" means it |

<Note>
When a workspace has no emoji set, its row shows the icon of its first saved tab — so workspaces tend to look like what they contain, automatically.
</Note>

## Folders

Once you're past a handful of workspaces, group them into **folders** ("Clients", "Personal", "Archive"…):

- **Create** — click the **New folder** icon at the top of the side panel's Workspaces list, or the **+** next to **Folders** in the dashboard sidebar. Type the name, press Enter.
- **Rename / Set emoji icon / Delete folder** — in the folder's **⋯** menu, on either surface. Deleting a folder is gentle: **the workspaces inside are moved out, not deleted**.
- **Reorder** — drag a folder up or down the list in the dashboard's expanded sidebar; a blue line shows where it'll land.
- **Fill them** — on the dashboard, drag a workspace onto a folder (or onto a folder's icon in the collapsed rail), use **⋯ → Move to folder**, or select several and move them together (below). Folders remember whether you left them open or closed.

## Select several at once (bulk actions)

On the dashboard's expanded sidebar you can act on many workspaces in one go:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start selecting">
    Hover any workspace or folder row — a faint checkbox appears on its left. Click it and you're in selection mode. Ticking a folder ticks everything inside it. **Select all** / **Deselect all** sits at the top of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on the selection">
    A bar appears at the bottom of the sidebar showing **"N selected"** with two buttons: **Move** (pick a folder, or **No folder (ungrouped)**) and **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Changed your mind?">
    **Esc** clears the selection. And after a bulk delete, a toast appears with an **Undo** button — click it within **5 seconds** and everything comes back exactly as it was.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Bulk-deleting a **folder** deletes the workspaces inside it too (unlike deleting a folder from its ⋯ menu, which moves them out first). The 5-second **Undo** on the toast is your safety net — after that, it's permanent.
</Warning>

## The tabs inside a workspace

Select a workspace on the dashboard and its middle **Tabs** pane lists every saved tab. From there:

- **Click a tab** — if this workspace is the one your window is using, you jump to the live tab; otherwise the page opens in a new tab.
- **Hover for actions** — **Rename tab** (your name shows everywhere, see [renaming](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#rename-any-tab)), **Open in new tab**, and **Remove tab**.
- **Drag to reorder** the saved list, or **drag a tab onto another workspace** in the sidebar to move it there.

<Warning>
**Remove tab** removes it from the workspace — and if that workspace is the active one, it also **closes the live browser tab**. There's no confirmation, so aim carefully.
</Warning>

Tabs are also saved automatically as you browse: with **Auto-save tabs** on (the default), the active workspace quietly keeps up with what you open and close, so switching away never loses anything.

## Resources — saved links {#resources-saved-links}

Every workspace also has **Resources**: links you want to *keep* without keeping a tab open. They live in the right-hand pane on the dashboard.

- **Add** — click **Add** in the Resources pane header, paste the address, optionally give it a title, **Save**.
- **From a tab** — click **Save as resource** on any row of the [Open Tabs view](/help/unscrambled-tabs/open-tabs#what-you-can-do-with-a-tab), or right-click any page → **Unscrambled Tabs** → **Save for later & close tab**.
- **Edit / Remove** — hover a resource for the pencil and ✕.

The magic is in the click: **click a resource and it opens in a new tab *and* graduates into the workspace's tabs** — it stops being a bookmark and starts being part of the session. (Want to peek without that? Hover and use **Open in new tab** instead, and the resource stays put.) When the AI's [Save to Workspaces](/help/unscrambled-tabs/ai-and-search#organise-your-tabs-with-a-nudge) mode or a [Workona import](/help/unscrambled-tabs/backup-import#workona-import) files resources for you, they arrive tidily grouped into named sections.

In the side panel, the active workspace's resources appear as a **Resources** section — click one there and it simply opens in a new tab.

## Pinned tabs

Tabs you pin in Chrome are first-class citizens: pinning survives every switch and restart, and pinned tabs are **never** suspended by [Performance modes](/help/unscrambled-tabs/performance-and-ram) or closed by [Close duplicates](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#close-duplicate-tabs-in-one-click).

## Opening a page you already have open?

Unscrambled Tabs spots it and offers to jump you to the existing tab instead — see [the duplicate catcher](/help/unscrambled-tabs/manage-tabs#already-open-the-duplicate-catcher).
